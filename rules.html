Server Rules

WireLineRP Community Guidelines

1. Respect & Community Conduct

Respect all players and staff. Harassment, hate speech, excessive toxicity, and targeted personal attacks are not allowed.

2. Stay In Character

Remain in character during roleplay situations. Avoid breaking character unless absolutely necessary.

3. No Random Deathmatch

Do not attack or kill players without a valid roleplay reason and proper interaction.

4. No Vehicle Deathmatch

Vehicles may not be intentionally used as weapons without a legitimate roleplay reason.

5. No Metagaming

Do not use information your character would not realistically know.

6. No Powergaming

Do not force unrealistic actions on other players or abuse game mechanics for an unfair advantage.

7. Value Your Life

Your character should react realistically to dangerous situations and serious threats.

8. Exploits & Cheating

Exploiting bugs, cheating, mod menus, or third-party software that provides an unfair advantage is prohibited.

9. Staff Decisions

Follow staff instructions during active situations. Appeals and complaints should be handled through the proper channels.

10. Use Common Sense

Not every possible situation can be written into the rules. Use good judgment and prioritize quality roleplay.
