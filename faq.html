Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about WireLineRP

Is WireLineRP free to play?

Yes. You will need FiveM and a legitimate copy of Grand Theft Auto V.

Is WireLineRP serious roleplay?

Yes. WireLineRP is built around serious roleplay, character development, and player-driven stories.

Can I own a business?

WireLineRP supports player-owned businesses. Availability and requirements depend on the business and server progression.

Can I join Police or EMS?

Yes. Emergency service positions may require an application, training, and approval from department leadership.

Where do I report a bug?

Report bugs through the appropriate WireLineRP Discord support channel.

Where can I get help?

Use the WireLineRP Discord support channels if you need assistance from staff.
